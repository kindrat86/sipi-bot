Limit types

Per-Agent Spend Limits

A per-agent limit is the agent's own budget — a ceiling it can't exceed regardless of what it's doing.

Daily Spend Limits

A daily spend limit is a rolling 24-hour ceiling — the budget that resets every morning, enforced all day.

Monthly Spend Limits

A monthly limit is the budget envelope for the month — best used as the outer layer above daily caps.
